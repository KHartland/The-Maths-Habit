# AQA-Style Image Prompts for GCSE Maths Questions

## AQA Exam Diagram Style Guide

AQA GCSE Maths papers use a distinctive, consistent visual style for all diagrams. To create authentic-looking supporting images, follow these characteristics:

### Core Visual Characteristics

1. **Black and white only** - No colour whatsoever. Pure black lines on white background.
2. **Simple line drawings** - Clean, precise geometric shapes with no shading, gradients, or fills.
3. **Thin, consistent line weight** - Uniform stroke width throughout (approximately 1pt).
4. **Sans-serif labelling** - Clean, simple font (like Arial) for all labels and measurements.
5. **Minimal decoration** - No artistic flourishes, shadows, or 3D effects.
6. **"Not drawn accurately"** - Diagrams are intentionally not to scale to prevent measurement-based answers.
7. **Right angle squares** - Small squares in corners to indicate 90° angles.
8. **Tick marks** - Equal-length sides marked with matching tick marks.
9. **Arrow notation** - Vectors and directions shown with simple arrow heads.
10. **Dotted/dashed lines** - Used for hidden edges, construction lines, or heights.

### Typography
- Labels: Capital letters (A, B, C, D...) at vertices
- Measurements: Numbers with units (8 cm, 45°, 3.5 m)
- Variables: Italic lowercase (x, y, h)
- Font: Sans-serif, regular weight, consistent size

### Layout
- Generous white space around diagrams
- Centred or left-aligned
- Scale appropriate for the page
- No background patterns or textures

## Foundation Tier Prompts

### N2 - Submarine Depth (Negative Numbers)
**Prompt:** Simple black and white line diagram showing a vertical cross-section of ocean. Horizontal line at top represents sea level, labelled "0m". Vertical scale on left showing depth markers: 0, -20, -40, -60, -80, -100. Simple submarine shape (oval with conning tower) drawn with thin black lines, no fill. Dotted arrow showing movement up 18m, solid arrow showing dive down. Depth labels: -45m, -27m, -81m at relevant positions. Clean technical diagram style, no shading. Text: "Not drawn accurately" in small font.

### N6 - Square Garden with Fence Posts
**Prompt:** Simple black and white line diagram showing a square viewed from above. Four sides of equal length with tick marks indicating equal sides. Small circles or dots representing fence posts, 8 evenly spaced along each side. Dimension arrow showing "12 m" along one side. Corner posts clearly visible. Clean geometric style, no fill or shading. Text: "Not drawn accurately" in small font.

### N7 - Storage Container with Crates
**Prompt:** Black and white isometric diagram of a large cube with a smaller cube inside. Larger cube drawn with solid lines, smaller cube with solid lines inside. Dimension arrows showing: large cube "5 m" on each visible edge, small cube "2 m" on each visible edge. Hidden edges shown as dashed lines. Clean technical drawing style, no shading. Text: "Not drawn accurately" in small font.

### N8 - Bus Stop Timing (LCM)
**Prompt:** Simple black and white diagram showing a timeline/number line. Horizontal arrow representing time from 9:00 AM onwards. Tick marks at regular intervals. Two rows of markers above the line: Row A with crosses every 12 minutes, Row B with circles every 18 minutes. Points where both align clearly marked. Labels: "Route A: every 12 min" and "Route B: every 18 min". Clean diagram style.

### A2 - Ball Trajectory
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate graph showing parabolic curve. Axes clearly labelled: vertical axis "h (metres)" with scale 0, 5, 10, 15, 20; horizontal axis "t (seconds)" with scale 0, 1, 2, 3, 4. Smooth parabola curve starting at origin, reaching maximum around t=2, returning to axis at t=4. Grid lines shown as thin grey or dotted lines. Key points may be marked with small crosses. Formula "h = 20t - 5t²" shown. Clean graph paper style.

### A5 - Cylindrical Water Tank
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram of a cylinder shown in 3D perspective. Solid ellipse for top, solid ellipse for bottom connected by two vertical lines. Dimension arrow across top showing "radius = 3 m". Dimension arrow along height showing "h = 7 m". Dashed line from centre of top to edge showing radius. Small right-angle square where radius meets edge. Clean technical drawing, no shading. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### A9 - Zipline Coordinates
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate grid. X-axis from 0 to 8, Y-axis from -2 to 8. Grid lines shown. Straight line segment from point A(2, 7) to point B(6, -1). Points marked with small crosses and labelled "A(2, 7)" and "B(6, -1)". Clean graph style, thin lines, no decoration.

### A15 - Quadratic Graph (Firework)
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate graph. X-axis from -2 to 4, Y-axis from -2 to 5. Parabola curve showing y = (x-3)(x+1), crossing x-axis at x = -1 and x = 3. Maximum point at (1, 4) may be marked. Grid lines as thin dotted lines. Axes with arrow heads. Clean mathematical graph style.

### A17 - Cinema Tickets
**Prompt:** Simple black and white diagram showing a price board or sign. Rectangle containing text: "TICKET PRICES", "Adult: £x", "Child: half price". Below, simple stick figures or icons representing 3 adults and 2 children. Text: "Total paid: £36". Clean, minimal line drawing style.

### A19 - Café Order (Simultaneous Equations)
**Prompt:** Simple black and white diagram showing two rows. Row 1: Three coffee cup icons + two tea cup icons = £13. Row 2: Two coffee cup icons + one tea cup icon = £8. Cups drawn as simple line drawings (circles with handles for cups, curved top for steam). Clean, minimal style.

### A21 - Height Restriction
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing a vertical measuring scale/ruler. Scale marked from 0 cm at bottom to 220 cm at top in increments. Horizontal bracket or line at 120 cm labelled "minimum". Horizontal bracket at 200 cm labelled "maximum". Shaded or hatched region between 120 and 200 showing valid range. Arrow pointing to valid region. Clean diagram style.

### A23 - Theatre Seating
**Prompt:** Black and white bird's eye view diagram of theatre seating rows. Curved or straight rows shown from above, getting progressively wider. Row 1 (front) labelled "15 seats", Row 2 labelled "19 seats", Row 3 labelled "23 seats", Row 4 labelled "27 seats". Small rectangles representing seats. Stage area at front shown as rectangle. Clean architectural plan style.

### R4 - Ratio Sharing
**Prompt:** Simple black and white diagram showing a bar model. Long rectangle divided into 9 equal parts. First 2 parts bracketed and labelled "Ann", next 3 parts labelled "Ben", final 4 parts labelled "Carl". Total shown as "£270" above the whole bar. Ratio "2 : 3 : 4" written below. Clean diagram style.

### R5 - Animal Shelter Ratio
**Prompt:** Simple black and white bar model diagram. Rectangle divided into 8 equal parts. First 3 parts labelled "Cats", remaining 5 parts labelled "Dogs". Total shown as "40 animals". Ratio "3 : 5" written below. Clean, minimal style.

### G1 - Cube Properties
**Prompt:** Black and white 3D diagram of a cube. All 12 edges drawn with solid lines for visible edges, dashed lines for hidden edges. Vertices labelled with letters A through H. One vertex marked with a small dot, one edge highlighted or marked, one face lightly indicated. Labels pointing to examples of "vertex", "edge", "face". Clean isometric style.

### G3 - Roundabout Angles
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing three straight lines meeting at a point (representing roads at a roundabout). Angles between lines marked with arcs. Two angles labelled "127°" and "85°". Third angle marked with "?" or "x". Small circle at centre representing roundabout. Clean geometric diagram. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G4 - Railway Crossing Angles
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing two straight lines intersecting (railway crossing road). One angle marked as "65°". All four angles at intersection shown with small arcs, three marked with "?". Clean line diagram. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G5 - Regular Polygon (Interior Angle)
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram of a regular polygon (15 sides, though exact shape less important than the labelling). One interior angle marked with an arc and labelled "156°". Clean geometric line drawing with equal sides indicated by tick marks. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G8 - Vector Translation
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate grid from 0 to 8 on both axes. Point P marked at (2, 5) with a cross and label. Arrow from P showing translation by vector, ending at (6, 2). Vector written as column vector notation. Arrow head clearly shown. Grid lines visible. Clean graph style.

### G9 - Reflection
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate grid. Triangle ABC with vertices at (1, 1), (3, 1), (2, 4) drawn with solid lines and labelled. Diagonal dashed line showing y = x. Reflected triangle A'B'C' shown on other side of line. Both triangles labelled with vertices. Clean geometric style.

### G11 - Circle with Chord
**Prompt:** Black and white circle diagram. Circle with centre O marked. Chord AB drawn as horizontal line. Radius from O marked as "10 cm". Perpendicular distance from O to chord marked with dashed line and labelled "8 cm". Right angle square where perpendicular meets chord. Length of chord marked with "?" or "x cm". Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G13 - Grain Silo (Cylinder + Cone)
**Prompt:** Black and white side elevation diagram of combined shapes. Cylinder on bottom: width marked as "radius = 3 m", height marked as "6 m". Cone on top: same base width, height marked as "4 m". Dashed line showing central axis. Clean technical drawing style, no shading. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G14 - Triangle on Coordinate Grid
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate grid. Triangle with vertices at (1, 2), (5, 2), (3, 6) drawn and labelled A, B, C. Grid squares visible. Axes labelled with scale. Each grid square represents 1 m noted. Clean graph style.

### G15 - Circle (Circumference to Area)
**Prompt:** Black and white circle diagram. Circle with centre O marked with a dot. Circumference labelled as "31.4 m" with an arrow along the curved edge. Radius line drawn with "r = ?" label. Clean geometric style. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G16 - Sector (Pizza Slice)
**Prompt:** Black and white sector diagram. Sector of circle with two radii and an arc. Radius labelled "15 cm" on both straight edges. Arc length labelled "12 cm" along curved edge. Angle at centre marked with "θ = ?". Clean geometric line drawing. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G19 - Ladder Against Wall (Pythagoras)
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing right-angled triangle formed by ladder against wall. Vertical line (wall), horizontal line (ground), diagonal line (ladder). Right angle square in corner. Ladder length: "5 m". Distance from wall: "1.5 m". Height on wall: "? m". Clean geometric diagram. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G20 - Angle of Elevation
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing trigonometry scenario. Vertical line representing tower/building. Horizontal line representing ground. Observer point 50 m from base (labelled). Dashed line from observer to top of building. Angle of elevation marked as "32°" at observer. Height of building marked as "h = ?". Clean technical diagram. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### P6 - Venn Diagram
**Prompt:** Black and white Venn diagram. Two overlapping circles inside a rectangle. Left circle labelled "Pizza", right circle labelled "Burgers". Regions labelled: left only section, overlap section, right only section, outside both sections. Numbers in each region. Rectangle labelled "30 students". Clean diagram style.

### P7 - Sample Space (Coins)
**Prompt:** Black and white tree diagram or sample space grid. If tree: Three levels showing H/T branches for three coin flips, with all 8 outcomes listed (HHH, HHT, HTH, etc.). If grid: 2×4 or organised list showing all outcomes. Clean diagram with clear labelling.

### P8 - Probability Without Replacement
**Prompt:** Black and white tree diagram. First branch: R (red) and B (blue) from a box. Second branches from each: showing reduced probabilities. Probabilities on branches: 4/10 and 6/10 for first pick, adjusted fractions for second pick. Outcomes listed at ends. Clean probability tree style.

### S4 - Distance-Time Graph
**Prompt:** Black and white graph on grid paper. Axes: horizontal "Time (hours)" from 0 to 2.5, vertical "Distance (km)" from 0 to 100. Line graph showing: steep line from (0,0) to (1,60), horizontal line from (1,60) to (1.5,60), steep line from (1.5,60) to (2,100). Grid lines visible. Clean graph style.

### S5 - Finding Unknown from Mean
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing 5 boxes or figures in a row. Four boxes contain numbers: 8, 10, 14, 15. Fifth box contains "?". Above or below: "Mean = 12". Arrow pointing to unknown value. Clean diagram style.

## Higher Tier Prompts

### A2-Higher - Box with Algebraic Dimensions
**Prompt:** Black and white 3D diagram of an open rectangular box. Three visible dimensions labelled with algebraic expressions: length "(2x + 3)", width "(x - 4)", height "(x + 1)". Dashed lines for hidden edges. Clean isometric technical drawing. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### A13-Higher - Quadratic Sequence Pattern
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing rows of dots or squares in triangular arrangement. Row 1: 5 dots, Row 2: 12 dots, Row 3: 23 dots, Row 4: 38 dots. Each row labelled with n value and number of dots. Pattern building upward. Clean mathematical diagram.

### A15-Higher - Simultaneous Equations Graph
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate graph. Parabola y = x² - 4x + 5 drawn as smooth curve. Straight line y = 2x - 3 drawn. Point of intersection marked with coordinates. Axes labelled, grid visible. Clean graph style.

### A17-Higher - Projectile Motion Graph
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate graph. Axes: horizontal "x (horizontal distance)", vertical "h (height in metres)". Inverted parabola starting at origin, reaching maximum, returning to x-axis. Maximum point marked. X-intercepts marked. Equation shown. Grid lines visible. Clean graph style.

### A21-Higher - Parabola Discriminant
**Prompt:** Black and white coordinate graph showing three different parabolas: one crossing x-axis twice (two real roots), one touching x-axis once (equal roots), one not touching x-axis (no real roots). Each labelled with discriminant condition: "b² - 4ac > 0", "b² - 4ac = 0", "b² - 4ac < 0". Clean mathematical diagram.

### A24-Higher - Football Trajectory
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing parabolic trajectory. Ground as horizontal line. Parabola from origin to landing point. Maximum height marked as "20 m" with dashed vertical line at "t = 2 s". Landing point at "t = 4 s". Axes: horizontal "t (seconds)", vertical "h (metres)". Clean diagram. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### R2-Higher - Workers and Time (Inverse Proportion)
**Prompt:** Black and white bar model or diagram. Rectangle showing wall divided into sections. First 3/8 shaded or marked as "completed in 3 days by 12 workers". Remaining 5/8 marked as "to be completed by 8 workers". Timeline below showing days. Clean diagram style.

### G1-Higher - Regular Nonagon
**Prompt:** Black and white regular 9-sided polygon (nonagon). One interior angle marked with arc and labelled "140°". All sides marked with tick marks to show equal length. Centre point may be marked. Clean geometric line drawing. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G3-Higher - Trapezium
**Prompt:** Black and white trapezium diagram. Parallel sides clearly shown as horizontal. Top side labelled "8 m", bottom side labelled "12 m". Height shown as dashed perpendicular line with "h = ?" label. Right angle squares where height meets parallel sides. Area noted as "50 m²". Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G5-Higher - Cone and Cylinder Comparison
**Prompt:** Black and white side-by-side diagram. Left: cylinder with radius r and height h labelled. Right: cone with same radius r and height h labelled. Both shown in 2D cross-section or 3D wireframe. Equal dimensions indicated. Cylinder volume "300π cm³" labelled. Cone volume "?" labelled. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G9-Higher - Sector (Radians)
**Prompt:** Black and white sector diagram. Two radii of length "12 cm" labelled. Arc of length "15 cm" labelled along curve. Angle at centre marked as "θ radians = ?". Dashed lines may show radius measurement. Clean geometric diagram. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G11-Higher - 3D Diagonal (Cuboid)
**Prompt:** Black and white 3D cuboid diagram. Dimensions: "3 cm", "4 cm", "12 cm" labelled on edges. Space diagonal drawn from one corner to opposite corner, shown as dashed line through interior. Diagonal labelled "d = ?". Hidden edges as dashed lines. Clean isometric diagram. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G15-Higher - Hemisphere on Cylinder
**Prompt:** Black and white composite shape diagram. Cylinder with hemisphere on top. Both share radius "6 cm" labelled. Cylinder height "10 cm" labelled. Hemisphere shown as semicircle in cross-section or as 3D. Dashed lines for hidden edges. Clean technical drawing. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### G19-Higher - Two Angles of Elevation
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram. Vertical building with window marked partway up and roof at top. Observer point 50 m from base. Two dashed lines of sight: one to window at angle "38°", one to roof at angle "62°". Angles marked at observer. Height of window marked as "h = ?". Clean technical diagram. Text: "Not drawn accurately".

### P5-Higher - Expected Value Probability
**Prompt:** Black and white table or diagram showing game outcomes. Column headers: "Outcome", "Probability", "Winnings". Rows: "Roll 6", "1/6", "+£10"; "Roll odd", "3/6", "+£2"; "Roll 2 or 4", "2/6", "-£3". Clean table format with grid lines.

### P6-Higher - Two-Stage Tree Diagram
**Prompt:** Black and white probability tree. First stage: "Box A" with branches to "Red (3/8)" and "Blue (5/8)". Second stage from each: "Box B" with updated probabilities. Final outcomes listed with calculated probabilities. Clean tree diagram with all branches labelled.

### S2-Higher - Box Plots Comparison
**Prompt:** Black and white diagram showing two box plots on same scale. Horizontal axis: "Score" from 0 to 100. Box plot 1 labelled "Class A": median at 62, IQR = 15. Box plot 2 labelled "Class B": median at 58, IQR = 24. Whiskers, quartiles, and medians clearly shown. Clean statistical diagram.

### S3-Higher - Scatter Graph with Regression Line
**Prompt:** Black and white scatter graph. Axes: horizontal "Hours studied (x)", vertical "Exam mark % (y)". Scatter of points showing positive correlation. Line of best fit drawn through points. Equation "y = 2.3x + 15" labelled. Grid visible. Clean graph style.

## Master Prompt Template for AI Image Generators

When generating AQA-style diagrams, use this template:

```
Create a black and white mathematical diagram in the style of a UK GCSE exam paper.

Key requirements:
- Pure black lines on white background (no colour, no shading, no fills)
- Clean, precise geometric shapes with thin, consistent line weight
- Sans-serif font for all labels (like Arial)
- Include "Not drawn accurately" in small text if it's a geometry diagram
- Use dashed lines for hidden edges or construction lines
- Small squares to indicate right angles
- Tick marks on equal-length sides
- Capital letters (A, B, C) for vertices
- Measurements with units (8 cm, 45°)
- Italic letters for variables (x, y)
- Generous white space around the diagram
- No decorative elements, shadows, or 3D effects

Specific diagram: [INSERT SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS]
```

Before using an generated image, verify:

- [ ] Black and white only (no colour)
- [ ] No shading or gradients
- [ ] Clean, thin line weight
- [ ] Clear, readable labels
- [ ] "Not drawn accurately" text (for geometry)
- [ ] Right angle squares where needed
- [ ] Appropriate use of dashed lines
- [ ] Sans-serif font throughout
- [ ] No decorative elements
- [ ] Sufficient white space
