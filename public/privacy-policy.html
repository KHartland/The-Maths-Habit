Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 March 2026

The Maths Habit ("we", "our", "the app") is operated by Parallel Education Ltd. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your display name and email address. As you use the app, we store your question responses, streaks, progress data and settings preferences. This data is necessary to provide the core functionality of the app, including tracking your revision progress and maintaining your position on school leaderboards.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the app experience, including: displaying your progress and streak data, showing your position on school leaderboards, sending optional daily revision reminders via push notifications, and maintaining your accessibility preferences.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, which uses industry-standard encryption and is hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure. Data is transmitted over HTTPS encryption.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Supabase — for authentication and data storage. Their privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

Google Sign-In — as an optional authentication method. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Push Notifications

The app may send daily reminder notifications to help you maintain your revision streak. You can disable these at any time through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The Maths Habit is designed for GCSE students (typically aged 14-16). We do not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is necessary for the app to function. We do not serve advertising or share data with advertisers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email address below.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also request a copy of your data in a portable format.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at: privacy@themathshabit.co.uk
