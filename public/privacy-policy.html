Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 February 2026

Overview

Square One Maths ("we", "us", "our") is a GCSE maths revision app built for students. We take your privacy seriously and collect only the minimum data needed to make the app work.

What We Collect

If you sign in with Google, we store your name, email address, and profile picture solely to identify your account and enable cloud sync across devices.

We store your practice data (questions answered, scores, streaks, and mastery levels) to track your progress. This data is linked to your account if you are signed in, or stored locally on your device if you are not.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any other personal information beyond what is described above.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the app's features: tracking your learning progress, syncing across devices, displaying streaks and leaderboards, and personalising your revision schedule using spaced repetition.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Supabase — for secure cloud storage of your account and practice data. Supabase stores data in EU data centres and complies with GDPR.

Google Sign-In — for authentication only. We receive your name, email, and profile picture. We do not access your Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

Vercel — for hosting the application.

Data Storage and Security

Your practice data is stored locally on your device using browser storage and, if signed in, synced to our cloud database hosted by Supabase. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Children's Privacy

Square One Maths is designed for GCSE students (typically aged 14-16). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. You can also export your data from the Settings page within the app.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
