Common Questions

How do I reset my password? Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and follow the instructions sent to your email.

How do streak freezes work? You earn a streak freeze for every 7-day streak milestone. A freeze automatically protects your streak if you miss a day.

Can I change my school? Go to Settings and update your school from the school selection option.
