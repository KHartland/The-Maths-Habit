# Image Generation Prompts for GCSE Maths Questions

These prompts are designed for AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, etc.) to create supporting visuals for exam-style questions. Images should be clean, educational, and suitable for teenagers.

---

## Foundation Tier - Number (N)

### N1 - Juice Bottles Comparison
**Question:** Comparing prices per litre of different juice bottles
**Prompt:** Four different sized juice bottles on a white shelf, clearly labelled "Small", "Medium", "Large", and "Family". Simple colourful fruit juice packaging, realistic product photography style, price tags visible on each. Clean supermarket shelf background. Educational diagram style, no text on bottles except size labels.

### N2 - Submarine Depth
**Question:** Submarine rising and diving

Blue gradient water from light at surface to dark at depth. Arrows showing upward and downward movement. Simple educational illustration style, clean lines.

### N6 - Square Garden with Fence Posts

## General Style Guidelines for All Images

1. **Clean and Educational**: Images should look professional and suitable for educational materials
2. **Clear Labels**: All measurements, coordinates, and mathematical notation should be clearly visible
3. **British Context**: Use British settings, spellings, and currency (£) where relevant
4. **Age-Appropriate**: Suitable for 14-16 year old GCSE students
5. **Minimal Clutter**: Focus on the mathematical concept without distracting elements
6. **Consistent Style**: Maintain a cohesive look across all images
7. **Colour Coding**: Use colour to distinguish different elements (e.g., red for unknowns, blue for given values)
8. **No Complex Text**: Keep text in images minimal - main content will be in the question itself
