## App Store Description

### Promotional Text (170 chars — can be updated without new build)
Build a daily GCSE maths habit with spaced repetition. 500+ AQA-mapped questions, streaks, worked solutions, and a leaderboard. Revision that sticks.

### Description
The Maths Habit helps GCSE students build a daily maths habit that actually works.

Every question is mapped to the AQA specification and uses spaced repetition — the same technique used by medical students — to make sure you remember what you've learned. The app adapts to you: topics you struggle with come back more often, while topics you've nailed fade into the background.

FEATURES:

• 500+ questions across 30+ GCSE topics, mapped to AQA Foundation and Higher tiers
• Spaced repetition scheduling that adapts to your strengths and weaknesses
• 5 difficulty levels per topic, from Grade 4 to Grade 9
• Daily streaks and streak freezes to keep you motivated
• Worked solutions and hints for every question
• Diagram-based questions for geometry, trigonometry, and circle theorems
• Cloud sync so your progress follows you across devices
• Leaderboard to compete with classmates
• Dark theme designed for comfortable study sessions
• Accessibility features including OpenDyslexic font and adjustable text size
• Works offline — practice anywhere, anytime

Whether you're aiming for a Grade 5 pass or pushing for a Grade 9, The Maths Habit gives you the daily practice that makes the difference.
