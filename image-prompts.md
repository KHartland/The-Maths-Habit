# Image Prompts for Square One Maths

67 diagram-dependent questions need images. Below are the prompts grouped by type, with the data you need to create for each image and the answer the question should produce.

---

## 1. Geometry — Angle Diagrams (G1)

### G1 Level 1 — "Work out the size of angle x" (3 images)

**Image 1 (v1):** Two straight lines crossing, or angles on a straight line. Label one angle and mark angle x. Choose values so that the answer is a clean number (e.g. angles on a straight line: one angle = 115°, so x = 65°).

**Image 2 (v2):** Angles around a point or vertically opposite angles. Label known angles and mark x. (e.g. vertically opposite: one angle = 132°, x = 132°).

**Image 3 (v3):** Angles at a point with three angles shown and x missing (e.g. 140° + 85° + x = 360°, x = 135°).

### G1 Level 4 — "Find the value of y/w/z with reasons" (3 images)

**Image 4 (v1):** Parallel lines with a transversal. Label one angle and mark y. Student must identify alternate/corresponding/co-interior angles and give reasons. (e.g. alternate angles, given = 72°, y = 72°).

**Image 5 (v2):** Parallel lines cut by a transversal, with an angle and w to find. (e.g. co-interior angles, given = 118°, w = 62°).

**Image 6 (v3):** Parallel lines with two transversals forming a triangle. Given two angles, find z with reasoning. (e.g. alternate angle = 55°, angle on straight line = 48°, z = 77°).

## 3. Geometry — Pythagoras' Theorem (G20)

### G20 Level 0 — "Calculate the length of the hypotenuse x" (3 images)

**Image 13 (v1):** Right-angled triangle with two shorter sides labelled (e.g. 3 cm and 4 cm). x is the hypotenuse. Answer: x = 5 cm.

**Image 14 (v2):** Right-angled triangle, sides 5 cm and 12 cm. Hypotenuse x = 13 cm.

**Image 15 (v3):** Right-angled triangle, sides 8 cm and 15 cm. Hypotenuse x = 17 cm.

### G20 Level 1 — "Find the missing shorter side y to 1 d.p." (3 images)

**Image 16 (v1):** Right-angled triangle with hypotenuse and one short side labelled (e.g. hyp = 10 cm, one side = 6 cm). y = √(100−36) = √64 = 8.0 cm.

**Image 17 (v2):** Hypotenuse = 13 cm, one side = 9 cm. y = √(169−81) = √88 ≈ 9.4 cm.

**Image 18 (v3):** Hypotenuse = 15 cm, one side = 7 cm. y = √(225−49) = √176 ≈ 13.3 cm.

### G20 Level 3 — "Use trig to find a side length" (3 images)

**Image 19 (v1):** Right-angled triangle with one angle (e.g. 35°) and one side (e.g. hypotenuse = 12 cm). Find BC using sin/cos. (e.g. BC = 12 × sin35° ≈ 6.9 cm).

**Image 20 (v2):** Right-angled triangle, angle = 42°, adjacent = 8 cm. Find QR = 8 × tan42° ≈ 7.2 cm.

**Image 21 (v3):** Right-angled triangle, angle = 28°, hypotenuse = 15 cm. Find XY = 15 × cos28° ≈ 13.2 cm.

### G20 Level 4 — "Find the angle to 1 d.p." (3 images)

**Image 22 (v1):** Right-angled triangle, opposite = 5 cm, adjacent = 8 cm. θ = tan⁻¹(5/8) ≈ 32.0°.

**Image 23 (v2):** Right-angled triangle, opposite = 7 cm, hypotenuse = 11 cm. x = sin⁻¹(7/11) ≈ 39.5°.

**Image 24 (v3):** Right-angled triangle, adjacent = 9 cm, hypotenuse = 14 cm. α = cos⁻¹(9/14) ≈ 50.0°.

## 9. Statistics — Frequency Tables (S3)

### S3 Level 3 — "Mode/median/mean from a frequency table" (3 images)

**Image 41 (v1):** Table: Number of pets (0, 1, 2, 3, 4) with frequencies for 20 families. Mode = the pets value with highest frequency. Total = Σ(pets × freq). (e.g. freqs: 4, 7, 5, 3, 1. Mode = 1, Total = 0×4+1×7+2×5+3×3+4×1 = 30).

**Image 42 (v2):** Table: Quiz scores (1, 2, 3, 4, 5) with frequencies. Mode and median required. (e.g. freqs: 2, 5, 8, 3, 2. Mode = 3, Median = 3).

**Image 43 (v3):** Table: Goals scored (0, 1, 2, 3) with frequencies totalling 15 matches. Mean = total goals ÷ 15. (e.g. freqs: 3, 5, 4, 3. Mean = (0+5+8+9)/15 = 22/15 ≈ 1.47).

### S3 Level 4 — "Estimated mean from grouped frequency table" (3 images)

**Image 44 (v1):** Grouped frequency table (e.g. Weight in kg: 0–10, 10–20, 20–30, 30–40 with frequencies). Student uses midpoints. (e.g. midpoints: 5, 15, 25, 35; freqs: 4, 8, 6, 2. Est. mean = (20+120+150+70)/20 = 18).

**Image 45 (v2):** Different grouped data (e.g. Time in minutes: 0–5, 5–10, 10–15, 15–20 with frequencies). (e.g. midpoints: 2.5, 7.5, 12.5, 17.5; freqs: 3, 7, 8, 2. Est. mean = (7.5+52.5+100+35)/20 = 9.75).

**Image 46 (v3):** Another grouped frequency table (e.g. Distance in km: 0–4, 4–8, 8–12, 12–16 with frequencies). (e.g. midpoints: 2, 6, 10, 14; freqs: 5, 9, 4, 2. Est. mean = (10+54+40+28)/20 = 6.6).
